# Privacy Policy for Arrow

**Effective Date:** September 2026

Arrow is an open-source arrow exit puzzle game. We value your privacy and have designed the application to collect **no personal data**.

## 1. No Personal Data Collection
Arrow does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, including but not limited to:
- Names, email addresses, or accounts
- IP addresses or device identifiers
- Behavioral analytics or tracking
- Geolocation data

## 2. Game Progress & Settings Stay on Your Device
The game records only your own **progress and settings** — such as levels cleared, stars earned, best times, the level you are in the middle of (so you can pick up where you left off), and your preferences (theme, music, sound effects, vibration, grid lines). This information:
- Is stored **locally on your device** only,
- Is used solely to **show it back to you** inside the app and provide a better gaming experience,
- Is **never sent to any server**, and
- Is **never shared with anyone** — not with us, not with third parties, ever.

Uninstalling the app removes this data from your device.

## 3. No Payments
Arrow contains no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or payments of any kind. We never ask for or process payment information.

## 4. No Ads or Third-Party Tracking
Arrow does not contain advertisements, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics scripts (such as Google Analytics).

## 5. No Internet Access
Arrow works entirely offline. The app does not request internet access and makes no network connections. The only device permission it uses is vibration, for haptic feedback while you play.

## 6. GDPR and CCPA Compliance
Since Arrow does not process, store, or share any personal data, it is inherently compliant with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). We have no user data to share, sell, or lose.

## 7. Open Source Transparency
The source code for Arrow is available for public inspection at: https://github.com/abhiabhi94/arrow-game. You are welcome to review the code to verify these privacy claims.

## 8. Contact
If you have any questions or concerns regarding this policy, please open an issue on our official GitHub repository.
